Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

TransferWatch is an independent project that shows live Durham Region Transit (DRT) bus arrivals. It is not affiliated with DRT or the Regional Municipality of Durham. This policy covers the TransferWatch website and the DRT Transit MCP server (the connector you can add to Claude and other AI assistants).

In short: there are no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, no ads, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared. We never ask for your name, email, or location.

What the DRT Transit connector receives

When an assistant uses the connector, it sends only the tool inputs needed to answer a transit question:

  • a stop search phrase (for example Oshawa Centre) or a stop ID,
  • optionally a route number (for example 902),
  • optionally how many minutes you need to walk to the stop.

The connector does not receive your conversation, your Claude account details, or your location. Requests arrive from the assistant provider's servers, not from your device.

How that information is used

Tool inputs are used only to look up stops and live arrivals and to calculate when to leave. The connector does not store your inputs, link them to you, or build any profile. Recent arrival results for a stop are cached in memory for about 15 seconds so that repeated requests don't overload the data feeds. Cached data contains bus information only.

The website

  • Saved stops and walk times are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • "Stops near me" uses your browser's location permission. Your position is used on your device to sort nearby stops and is never sent to our servers.
  • When you open a stop, your browser requests that stop's arrivals by stop ID.

Hosting and logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and the live data service runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS Lambda). These providers may keep standard operational records such as request time, duration, and errors, under their own privacy policies (GitHub, AWS). TransferWatch's own logs record service errors only and are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Data sources

Schedules and real-time bus data come from DRT's public GTFS and GTFS-Realtime feeds. Predictions may be wrong. Please allow extra time when it matters.

Children

The service collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
